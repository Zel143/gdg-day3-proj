🚀 The Quest Categories
Choose your path. 

The Agentic Architect (Tech Focused)
Build AI agents that perform tasks autonomously. Your system shouldn't just talk; it should do.
Focus: Tool calling, task planning, and multi-step reasoning.
Requirement: Demonstrate the agent interacting with an external API or data structure.

Local Impact Explorer (Community Focused)
Solve a problem unique to the Philippines or Southeast Asia (e.g., "Paano maiiwasan ang traffic sa EDSA" logic).
Focus: Hyper-local context and specific user pain points.

Requirement: A clear problem statement explaining why "Global AI" fails here.

The Chaos-to-Code Converter (Wildcard)
Automate the "unnecessary." Turn a hilarious daily annoyance or a random Filipino meme into a functional, automated workflow.
Focus: Creativity, humor, and automation of "chaos."

Requirement: Must automate at least a 3-step workflow using AI decision-making.
