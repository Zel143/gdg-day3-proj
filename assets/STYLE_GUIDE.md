# The Canon: Style & Tone Guide for Contributors

To ensure the integrity of the **Canon Framework**, all "Witnesses" (submissions) must pass the **Guardian AI Review**. This guide defines the linguistic and logical standards required for approval.

## 1. The Secular Scripture Tone
We do not use religious language, but we maintain "Scriptural Weight." This means your writing should feel like a timeless law or a fundamental observation of the universe.

| Avoid (Soft/Religious) | Use (Authoritative/Secular) |
| :--- | :--- |
| "I believe..." | "It is observed that..." |
| "We should try to..." | "Systemic integrity requires..." |
| "A miracle happened." | "An anomaly has converged into resonance." |
| "Fixing the bug." | "Resolving the inherent entropy." |
| "Good/Bad" | "Coherent/Incoherent" |

## 2. Structural Requirements
- **Declarative Sentences:** Avoid "maybe," "perhaps," or "I think." State your observation as a fact.
- **Lexicon Integration:** You **MUST** use at least one term from the active Lexicon. The AI will reject submissions that ignore our shared language.
- **Narrative Logic:** 
    - If you are writing a **Foundation (Genesis)**, it must be a "First Principle."
    - If you are writing a **Friction (Lamentations)**, you must describe the *logical gap* clearly.
    - If you are writing a **Coherence (Gospels)**, you **MUST** link it to a specific Friction entry.

## 3. The "Guardian" Review Workflow
1. **Draft:** Write your entry in the Submission Form.
2. **Review:** Click "Review with AI."
3. **Analyze:** Read the Guardian's feedback. If it says `REVISION REQUIRED`, pay close attention to the "Suggested Refinement."
4. **Iterate:** Edit your text until the Guardian provides the `[APPROVED]` verdict.
5. **Publish:** Only then will your Witness be etched into the global Firestore Canon for the whole team to see.

## 4. Examples of Approved Writing
> **Foundation:** "The preservation of data continuity is the primary axiom of systemic survival."
> 
> **Friction:** "Current fragmentation in user-identity protocols creates a state of high entropy, preventing cross-platform resonance."
